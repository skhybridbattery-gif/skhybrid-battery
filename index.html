Everything you need to know about hybrid battery replacement, warranty, prices, and ABS repair.

We provide an industry-leading 5-Year Official Written Warranty on all our hybrid battery replacements. It covers individual cell voltage balancing, overall battery pack performance, and any internal manufacturing defect. If any issue arises, our mobile team repairs or replaces it free of charge.

Lithium batteries are modern generation packs that are up to 60% lighter, run substantially cooler in Karachi's intense summer heat, and deliver a 20% to 30% improvement in fuel economy (up to 30-34 km/L on Aqua). Standard NiMH batteries are reliable OEM replacements. Both options come with our complete 5-year warranty.

Yes. ABS failure (hard brake pedal, continuous motor buzzing, or ABS/VSC warning lights on dashboard) is a very common issue in Toyota Aqua, Prius, and Honda Vezel. We specialize in ABS pump rebuilding, accumulator module replacement, electronic sensor calibration, and brake bleeding.
